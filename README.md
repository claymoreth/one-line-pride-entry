One line of earned pride: an old carpenter runs his thumb along a joint
he has cut a hundred times — this time it fits, and he smiles at no one
in particular. A single line of 131 characters (within the 1–240
character limit), written by `main.py` to `entry.txt` in the output
directory. No trophy, no podium, no graduation — just a quiet moment of
pride earned by effort, felt inside the line itself, landing in one read.
